Staying Power
How Standard Practice Creates Lasting Impact for Every Student and Every Teacher
James Smith
The new book from James Smith

Building schools where every student grows and every teacher stays.

Education already has standards for what students should learn. It has never had a standard practice for how schools do the work of teaching them. Staying Power shows what that looks like, one lesson, one role and one school at a time.

Get it on Amazon What's inside

I love teachers and administrators, because they are my family. From the prologue

A family moves across the district in the middle of October. On Monday their daughter walks into a new fourth-grade classroom, and nothing about it is strange. Same reading routine. Same way of showing what she knows. Same weekly note home. Her new teacher already has that day's lesson ready, and spent Sunday with her own family instead of building one from scratch.

That's not a dream. It's what happens when a school system has a standard practice, the way hospitals and engineers do: a bar everyone meets, not a mold that makes everyone the same.

About the author

James Smith

James has spent twenty-plus years building software for education, from Texas to California to Virginia and as far as Australia, working alongside hundreds of teachers and dozens of administrators. Outside education, he built inventory and personnel tracking for some of the country's largest retailers, where he learned that bad data is worse than no data. His wife, Melissa, has spent more than twenty years in education as a teacher, assistant principal and principal, and the software at the heart of this book carries her name.

He is also the author of Stop Banning What You Should Be Teaching, the educator's handbook for navigating AI in the classroom.
