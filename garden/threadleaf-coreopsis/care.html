Threadleaf Coreopsis

Coreopsis verticillata

Plant & Info Care Instructions

How to care for it

  • Low maintenance.
  • Thrives best in full sun, 6 to 8 hours every day.
  • Handles poor, rocky, or sandy soil.
  • 1 inch of water every week.
  • Little to no fertilizer needed.
  • Needs good soil drainage.
  • Wet or soggy soil harms the plant. Water regularly until the roots take hold.
