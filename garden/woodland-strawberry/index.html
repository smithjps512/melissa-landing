Woodland Strawberry

Fragaria vesca

Plant & Info Care Instructions

About this plant

  • The woodland strawberry (Fragaria vesca) is a flower in the rose family.
  • It occurs naturally across the Northern Hemisphere.
  • It is also known as the wild strawberry and the wood strawberry.
  • It has dark green leaves and bright red fruits.
  • The fruits are small yet flavorful.
