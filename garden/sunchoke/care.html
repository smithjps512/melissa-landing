Sunchoke (Jerusalem Artichoke)

Helianthus tuberosus

Plant & Info Care Instructions

How to care for it

  • Plant in a 5- to 15-gallon container or heavy grow bag — never straight into a greenhouse bed, or they will take over.
  • Use a loose, well-draining mix of potting soil, compost, and a little sand or perlite.
  • Plant tubers 3 to 5 inches deep with the eyes facing up, spaced about 12 inches apart.
  • Water thoroughly after planting, then mulch 3 inches deep with straw or leaves.
  • Fall: keep the greenhouse ventilated. They like 65–90°F. Water when the top 2 inches are dry — never waterlogged.
  • Winter: do not heat the greenhouse. They need a real chill to break dormancy and sweeten. Water the bare minimum.
  • Late winter: as daylight grows, shoots appear. Increase watering once they do.
  • Spring: after the last frost, move containers outside to full sun — they will outgrow the greenhouse ceiling.
