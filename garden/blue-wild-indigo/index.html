Blue Wild Indigo

Baptisia australis

Plant & Info Care Instructions

Fast facts

Plant type
Herbaceous perennial
Bloom time
Late spring to early summer (May–June)
Mature size
3 to 4 feet tall and wide
Hardiness zones
3–9
Sun
Full sun
Soil
Dry to medium moisture, well-draining

About this plant

  • Showy in pollinator gardens.
  • Blooms blue in late spring.
  • Has a shrubby appearance — forms a bushy mound with blue-green, clover-like leaves.
  • Host plant for six butterfly species, including the wild indigo duskywing.
  • Attractive black seed pods that rattle in the wind.
  • Named "false indigo" because colonial settlers used it to make a blue dye that was weaker than true indigo.
  • Fixes nitrogen in the soil — it belongs to the pea family.
Careful!Contains toxic alkaloids — do not eat any part of this plant.
