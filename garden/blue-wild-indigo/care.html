Blue Wild Indigo

Baptisia australis

Plant & Info Care Instructions

How to care for it

  • Flowers best in full sun.
  • Can take several years to reach full height — 3 to 4 years to reach full size.
  • Deep taproot makes it drought tolerant over time, and deer resistant once established.
  • Choose the permanent spot carefully — the long taproot means it dislikes being moved.
  • From seed, the hard seed coat needs scarification (sandpaper or an overnight warm-water soak).
  • Almost no maintenance once established. Stems die back to the ground in winter.
