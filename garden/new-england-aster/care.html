New England Aster

Symphyotrichum novae-angliae

Plant & Info Care Instructions

How to care for it

  • Sunlight: prefers full sun, though it tolerates light partial shade.
  • Soil: adapts to clay, loam, or sandy soil, but prefers rich, moist, well-draining conditions.
  • Water: needs medium to moist soil. It tolerates short dry spells once established.
  • Pinch back the stems 1 to 2 inches in late spring so the plant grows bushier and is less likely to flop over.
  • Plant it behind shorter perennials — the lower leaves brown and drop by flowering time.
  • Space plants 1 to 2 feet apart for airflow, to prevent fungal leaf diseases.
  • Divide large clumps every 2 to 3 years in spring to keep the plant vigorous.
