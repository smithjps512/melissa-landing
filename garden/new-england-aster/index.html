New England Aster

Symphyotrichum novae-angliae

Plant & Info Care Instructions

Fast facts

Native range
Central and eastern North America
Plant type
Herbaceous perennial
Mature size
2 to 6 feet tall, 1 to 3 feet wide
Bloom time
Late summer through October, until the first hard frost
Flower color
Purple, pink, lavender, or white with orange-yellow centers
Hardiness zones
Zones 4–8

About this plant

  • Formerly known as Aster novae-angliae.
  • It blooms late — one of the last big nectar sources before frost.
  • It needs at least 6 to 8 hours of direct sunlight each day to grow and bloom its best.
