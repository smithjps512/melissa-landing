Virginia Spiderwort

Tradescantia virginiana

Plant & Info Care Instructions

About this plant

  • Virginia spiderwort is edible, but parts of it are poisonous.
  • The flowers come in three colors: blue, pink, or white.
  • They grow and thrive in moist woods, stream banks, clearings, and roadsides.
  • Spiderwort is native to the eastern and east-central regions of North America.
