Virginia Spiderwort

Tradescantia virginiana

Plant & Info Care Instructions

How to care for it

  • Water: keep the soil moist, and water deeply if it is dry — about 1 inch per week.
  • Avoid soggy soil. Make sure the plant drains well.
  • Give it partial shade, about 4 to 6 hours a day.
  • Good drainage matters.
  • If the soil is healthy, fertilizer will rarely be needed.
  • Prune to prevent a floppy look.
