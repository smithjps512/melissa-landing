Tickseed

Coreopsis

Plant & Info Care Instructions

How to care for it

  • Sunlight: at least 6 to 8 hours of direct sun for the best blooms.
  • Soil: well-drained, lean, or sandy. It tolerates poor soil.
  • Water: an adult tickseed needs only about one inch of water a week. Seedlings need consistent watering until their roots take hold.
  • Height depends on the variety — a regular tickseed is around 3 feet, a dwarf can be as short as 8 inches, and a tall one can grow taller still.
