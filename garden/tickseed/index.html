Plant & Info Care Instructions

About this plant

  • A yellow, daisy-like flower.
  • It attracts bees, butterflies, and other pollinators.
  • The tickseed is a low-maintenance wildflower.
  • Why is it called "tickseed"? The name comes from the flower's seeds — they look like ticks. But the tickseed does not attract ticks.
