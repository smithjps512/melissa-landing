Black-Eyed Susan

Rudbeckia hirta

Plant & Info Care Instructions

How to care for it

  • Plant them in full sun so they get at least six hours of sun.
  • Use loose, well-draining soil — they dislike sitting in soggy soil.
  • Water plants regularly during the growing season.
