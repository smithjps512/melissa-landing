Rhododendron

Rhododendron spp.

Plant & Info Care Instructions

About this plant

  • Rhododendrons are popular, colorful spring flowers, famous for their beautiful blooms.
  • They are low maintenance.
  • Rhododendrons are evergreen or deciduous shrubs.
  • They get big — up to 20 feet tall and 15 feet wide.
  • They are toxic to humans, pets, and livestock.

History

  • Rhododendrons date back over 55 million years.
  • Their recorded human history spans ancient honey mishaps and 19th-century plant hunting.
