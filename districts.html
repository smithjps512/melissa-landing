Why it matters

Great teachers, no playbook.

In most districts, every teacher builds their own curriculum, their own grading and their own way of talking to families, mostly alone and mostly from scratch. Every principal invents their own way to run a school. Students feel it when they move from one class to the next, and the people who feel it most are the educators rebuilding what someone down the hall already built.

Bad data is worse than no data. Nobody decides on no data. Bad data that everyone trusts leads to bad decisions.

What your district gets

Everything a whole system needs, built around the teacher's day.

A teacher who has used Melissa for Teachers recognizes every screen. What changes is who else can help, by fixed and published rules.

📅

A prepared year for every course

Every school day has a ready lesson before the first day, aligned to state standards and laid out on your district's calendar, with on-level, advanced and Pre-AP/AP versions. Teachers can teach it as is, make it their own, or opt out.

📏

One yardstick, trusted measures

Shared checks and exit tickets in every class, so results compare like for like. Leaders see school and course roll-ups used for support, never a ranking of individual teachers.

🏡

Families in the loop

A short weekly note home in the family's language, drafted by Melissa and finished by the teacher, plus the Family Compass and Family Navigator so every family can see where their child stands.

🧭

Views for every role

Principals see where to coach. Program management sees how fully each school runs the system. The superintendent and board get one scorecard. The closer someone is to the child, the more detail they see.

🔗

Connected to what you already use

Melissa connects to your student information system, which stays the official record. Rosters come in, and the goal is that nothing gets entered twice. Google sign-in keeps working alongside.

🎓

Training that stays in the district

Onboarding for every adult, then certified trainers at two levels, district implementer and school coach, so your own people keep Standard Practice running in every building.

Teachers first

Built for teachers, not around them.

The district buys it, but teachers decide whether it lives, because they use it every day. So the rules about who sees what are fixed, published and simple.

  • Class Pulse, where students grade the class, stays private to the teacher. Only the teacher can choose to share it.
  • AI drafts and people decide. No grade, placement or referral is made by AI alone.
  • Results travel up as roll-ups for support, never as a list of individual teachers.
  • Results for very small groups are hidden to protect students.
  • A FERPA and COPPA data agreement comes before any student data.
How it starts

From the first conversation to every building.

We walk through it with you, until your own people run Standard Practice without us.

  1. A conversation about your district

    We meet with your leadership, and then your board and your teachers. Together we write down what success will look like on your district's own measures, before anyone signs.

  2. One campus first

    Your first campus launches with everything it needs, at no cost: a program manager, onboarding for every adult, the prepared year loaded, the Family Compass, and starting measures so everyone can see the change.

  3. Board adoption

    Standard Practice becomes board policy in a public vote, so the system outlasts any leadership change. No other school starts until then.

  4. District-wide rollout

    Every school runs the same way, with the program manager in place and certified trainers in every building.

No program manager yet? We can serve in that role while you hire, then hand it to your own certified person.
